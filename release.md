## 🌟 Overview & Highlights
Welcome to the official **v1.0.0 release** of **Zombie Assassin Mod**! This update introduces a brand new custom dimension (**The Diamond Realm**), ocean-spawning custom structures (**Tower 1** and **Netherite Pillars**), an automated **10x10 Trap Room** mechanism, cooked weapons, multi-tools, interactive display blocks, and a dedicated custom Creative Tab.

---

## 🎮 Feature Guide & How to Use

### 1. 💎 The Diamond Realm (Custom Dimension)
* **Description**: A whole new dimension structured like the Overworld terrain, but where the ground and underground layers are converted into **Netherite Blocks** and **Diamond Blocks**.
* **Accessing the Dimension**:
  * Use the **Dimensional Key** item from the custom creative tab (or `/give @s zombieassasian:dimensional_key`).
  * Right-click to open a portal/gateway, or use `/tp @s ~ ~ ~ zombieassasian:diamond_realm`.

### 2. 🏰 Custom Structures (Tower 1 & Netherite Pillars)
* **Description**: Ancient structures that spawn naturally along ocean coasts and beaches in both the Overworld and the Diamond Realm.
* **Finding Structures**:
  * Run `/locate structure zombieassasian:tower1` to locate the nearest **Tower 1**.
  * Run `/locate structure zombieassasian:netherite_pillar` to locate nearby **Netherite Pillars**.

### 3. ⚡ 10x10 Trap Room Mechanism (`trap_room_core`)
* **Description**: An automated trap system that crushes intruders with falling anvils.
* **How to Build & Activate**:
  1. Obtain the **Trap Room Core** block from the custom creative tab.
  2. Construct a **full 10x10 boundary** (all 36 border blocks) out of `Trap Room Core` blocks.
  3. *Note*: Placing single blocks or incomplete boundaries remains completely inert.
  4. Once the complete 10x10 boundary is sealed, the trap room becomes active.
  5. Any player, mob, or creature that steps inside the 10x10 room interior takes **3 hearts (6.0 damage)** and triggers falling anvils directly overhead. Anvils are hard-clamped to fall strictly inside the room interior.

### 4. ⚔️ Cooked Gear & Multi-Tool
* **Cooked Shield**: High-durability shield for defensive combat.
* **Cooked Diamond Axe**: Heavy-hitting axe with enhanced block breaking speed.
* **Wooden Multi-Tool**: An all-in-one survival tool combining axe, pickaxe, shovel, and hoe functionalities into a single inventory slot.

### 5. 🪟 Interactive Display Block
* **Description**: Place any item onto the Display Block to showcase rare loot in your base.

### 6. 🪣 Long-Ranged Bucket
* **Description**: Extended-reach bucket allowing liquid collection from longer distances.

### 7. 🐶 Cute Puppy Spawn Egg
* **Description**: Spawns a cute companion mob to accompany players on adventures.

### 8. 🎨 Custom Creative Tab
* All items and blocks added by this mod can be found in the **Zombie Assassin Mod** creative tab near the bottom of the creative inventory.

## 🛠️ Installation Instructions

1. Download and install **Minecraft Forge 1.20.1** (Recommended Version: `47.2.0` or higher).
2. Download `zombieassasian-1.0.0.jar` from the release assets.
3. Open your Minecraft directory:
   * **Windows**: `%appdata%\.minecraft`
   * **Mac**: `~/Library/Application Support/minecraft`
   * **Linux**: `~/.minecraft`
4. Move `zombieassasian-1.0.0.jar` into the `mods` folder.
5. Launch Minecraft using the Forge 1.20.1 profile!
